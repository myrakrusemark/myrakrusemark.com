Don't Tap the Bunny: privacy policy

TopoCo Art · last updated November 1, 2026

Don't Tap the Bunny collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't ask for internet access, so it can't send anything anywhere.

Display over other apps. The app asks for this permission so the rabbit can appear on top of whatever you're using. It draws the rabbit and notices taps on the rabbit itself. It can't see or read anything else on your screen.

Notifications. While the rabbit is enabled, Android shows a notification so you can see the app is running and turn it off.

Settings. Your on/off choice and how often the rabbit visits are stored on your phone. If you use Android's own backup, Android may include them in your Google backup. We never receive them.

Children. The app isn't aimed at children. It shows blood when the rabbit pops.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
